Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data — and the rights you have over it. We keep this plain and honest, the same way we run fulfilment.

On this page

Last updated: 9 June 2026

In short: we only collect the details you give us through our forms, call bookings and emails, we use them to prepare quotes and provide fulfilment services, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to show, correct or delete it at any time.

01Who we are

This website is operated by Pulse Fulfilment Limited ("Pulse Fulfilment", "we", "us" or "our"), a company registered in England & Wales. We provide order fulfilment and third-party logistics (3PL) services to ecommerce brands.

For the purposes of UK data protection law — the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 — Pulse Fulfilment Limited is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

02The data we collect

We collect only the personal data we need to respond to you and deliver our services. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

CategoryExamples
Contact & identityYour name, business name, job role, email address and phone number.
Enquiry detailsYour monthly order volume, sector, sales channels and carriers, and anything you tell us about your products and fulfilment needs.
Booking informationThe date and time you select for a discovery call, made through our scheduling provider.
Technical & usageInformation your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, device and browser type, and how you use the site (where analytics or cookies are enabled).
CommunicationsThe content of emails, calls and messages you exchange with us.

We do not intentionally collect special category data (such as health or political information) through this website. Please don't include it in free-text fields.

03How we collect it

  • Directly from you — when you complete our "Book a call" or "Get pricing" forms, request a quote, email or phone us, or book a discovery call.
  • Automatically — when you browse the site, through cookies and similar technologies (see Cookies).
  • From third parties — occasionally from referral partners or publicly available business sources, where you've engaged with us through them.

04Why we use it & our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR we must have a lawful basis to process your personal data. We rely on the following:

What we doLawful basis
Respond to enquiries, prepare bespoke quotes and arrange discovery callsLegitimate interests / steps to enter into a contract
Provide and manage our fulfilment services once you become a clientPerformance of a contract
Send service updates and relevant marketing (where you'd expect it or have opted in)Consent and/or legitimate interests
Keep records, meet legal, tax and accounting obligationsLegal obligation
Maintain the security and performance of our websiteLegitimate interests

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we've considered whether those interests are overridden by your rights, and you can object at any time (see Your rights). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

05Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed. These include:

  • Scheduling — our booking calendar is powered by Calendly. When you book a call, the details you provide are processed by Calendly to manage your appointment.
  • Website hosting & forms — our hosting and form-handling providers store and transmit submissions so they reach us.
  • Analytics — where enabled, providers that help us understand site usage in aggregate.
  • Professional advisers & carriers — accountants, IT and delivery/carrier partners where required to provide our services.
  • Authorities — where we're legally required to disclose information.

All processors are bound by contracts requiring them to protect your data and use it only on our instructions.

06International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place — such as a UK adequacy decision, the International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses — so your data receives equivalent protection.

07How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes we collected it for:

  • Enquiries that don't become clients — typically up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Client records — for the duration of our relationship and for as long as needed afterwards to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements (usually 6 years).

08How we keep it secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access — including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and working only with reputable providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take security seriously and review our measures regularly.

09Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how we use your data (this policy).
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data erased ("right to be forgotten"), where applicable.
  • Restrict or object to our processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, email us at sales@pulsefulfilment.co.uk. We'll respond within one month. There's normally no charge.

10Cookies

Cookies are small files placed on your device. We use essential cookies that make the site work, and — where enabled — analytics cookies that help us understand how the site is used. Some features, such as our embedded booking calendar, may set their own cookies when you use them.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

11Children

Our website and services are aimed at businesses and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. We'll post any changes here and update the "last updated" date above. Please check back periodically.
