How to choose a 3PL that actually grows with you

The Pulse Fulfilment Team 4 June 2026 5 min read

Outsourcing fulfilment is one of the highest-leverage decisions a growing brand makes — and one of the easiest to regret. The right partner disappears into the background while your orders fly out the door. The wrong one becomes a second job. Here's how to tell them apart before you sign anything.

Start with where you're going, not where you are

Most brands pick a 3PL for the volume they ship today. Then they double, the cracks appear, and they're re-platforming a year later — migrating stock, re-integrating channels and re-training a team mid-peak. Painful, and avoidable.

Ask instead: can this partner still fit when I'm shipping five times more? A 3PL built for scale should handle your first 300 orders a month and your 30,000th without you ever feeling a ceiling. If a provider only talks about your current numbers, that's a warning sign.

Onboarding speed tells you everything

How a 3PL onboards you is the clearest preview of how they'll operate forever. Slow, vague, lots of forms and no named contact? That's the relationship you're buying. Fast, hands-on, someone who checks your first orders actually flow through correctly? Same.

A good onboarding maps your channels, books your stock in properly, and runs test orders before you go live. It should take days, not months.

How does the pricing actually scale?

Cheap-looking per-order rates often hide the real cost — look for pricing that's transparent and tiered to volume. You want a partner whose incentives line up with yours.

And watch how they quote. A good 3PL digs into your actual products — their weights, sizes and how fiddly they are to pack — not just "how many SKUs do you have?" before reeling off a price. Those details decide how many orders can realistically be picked and packed an hour, and that's what genuinely drives the cost. A partner asking the right questions up front is one who'll price you fairly and handle your products properly.

The best 3PL relationship is one where their growth and yours point in the same direction.

Can they reach your customers — and your channels?

Two practical checks. First, carriers: a single courier means a single point of failure and no leverage on rate or speed. A partner that rate-shops across a dozen-plus carriers protects both your delivery promise and your margin. Second, integrations: every sales channel you use — Shopify, Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, your ERP — should connect cleanly, with orders and tracking flowing automatically. No spreadsheets, no double entry.

You're hiring a team, not a warehouse

Software and shelving are table stakes. What you're really buying is judgement — the people who spot a mislabelled SKU before it ships, who flag that you're about to run out of stock before peak, who pick up the phone when something's gone sideways.

Take this to your shortlist call

Ask each provider: How fast is onboarding, start to live? What's the all-in cost per order at my current and 5× volume? How many carriers do you rate-shop across? Which of my exact sales channels do you integrate with? And — who, by name, will I actually speak to when something goes wrong?

Get straight answers to those five and you'll know very quickly whether a 3PL is built to grow with you, or just to take this month's orders.
