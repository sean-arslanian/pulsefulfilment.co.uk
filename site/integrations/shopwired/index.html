How it works

Connecting Shopwired to Pulse

1

Connect your store

Add the Pulse connector or your API keys to your Shopwired store. Setup takes minutes, with no developer required.

2

Orders flow into Pulse

Every Shopwired order drops straight into our warehouse system in real time and enters the pick queue.

3

Stock & tracking sync back

Inventory levels and tracking numbers write back to Shopwired automatically, so your store always shows the truth.
