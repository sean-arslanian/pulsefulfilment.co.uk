Serving the West Midlands

Built for Birmingham ecommerce brands

From the Jewellery Quarter's makers to the city's fast-growing DTC brands, Birmingham businesses need fulfilment that handles a real mix of products. We pick, pack and route every order accurately, matching each parcel to the best carrier for cost and speed.

Birmingham sits squarely on the motorway network, roughly 100 miles from our Wallasey hub, so next-working-day delivery is standard for orders placed before our 2pm cut-off. The West Midlands has a hugely varied ecommerce base — jewellery and luxury goods, electronics, fashion and food brands — and our systems are built to handle high-value, serial-tracked and fragile items alongside everyday lines. Every Birmingham order flows into one place, gets picked with 99.8% accuracy and ships out branded.
